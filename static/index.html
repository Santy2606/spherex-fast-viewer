The Scientific Challenge

Time-Domain Astronomy at Infrared Scale

Too much data for any one person to inspect. Objects that shift position or brightness between visits are hidden in catalogs of billions of sources.

🌑

Cold Brown Dwarfs

Faint and extremely red objects like WISE 0855 drift across the sky and are easy to miss in static catalogs.

💫

High Proper-Motion Stars

Stars like Barnard's Star shift position between epochs. Blinking aligned images is the classic way to catch them.

🔴

Variable & Young Stars

T Tauri stars and Mira giants change brightness from one epoch to the next, so a single snapshot never tells the whole story.
