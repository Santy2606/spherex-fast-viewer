The Scientific Challenge

Time-Domain Astronomy at Infrared Scale

Modern sky surveys generate terabytes of multi-epoch imagery. The challenge: efficiently detect and characterize moving, variable, or transient objects hidden within massive catalogs.

🌑

Cold Brown Dwarfs

Sub-stellar objects like WISE 0855 emit primarily in the mid-infrared. SPHEREx's NIR sensitivity enables unprecedented detection of these elusive cold worlds across all-sky epochs.

💫

High Proper-Motion Stars

Stars like Barnard's Star shift position measurably across epochs. Multi-epoch blink comparison is the gold standard technique for identifying these rapid movers within dense star fields.

🔴

Variable & Young Stars

T Tauri variables, Mira giants, and eruptive young stellar objects show dramatic brightness changes. The SPHEREx time baseline provides a new window into these stellar life-cycle events.

About the Mission

SPHEREx — NASA's Cosmic Survey

The Spectro-Photometer for the History of the Universe, Epoch of Reionization and Ices Explorer is NASA's newest wide-field infrared survey telescope. It maps the entire sky four times over two years, collecting spectra in 102 channels from 0.75 to 5 microns.
